About us

Radical transparency, from farm gate to your roaster.

Our mission is to give roasters of every size exceptional green coffee, with nothing hidden about where it came from or what it cost.

Our story

Started because the invoices didn't add up

We were small-batch roasters ourselves, and tired of green coffee sold with a story but no numbers behind it. So we started buying direct: same lots the big players get, sold in quantities a two-person roastery can actually use.

Every lot on this site ships with the price we paid the producer, the cupping score, and how it got here. If we wouldn't roast it ourselves, we don't list it.
